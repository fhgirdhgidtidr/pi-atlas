INSIDE THE ATLAS

One number.
A billion places.

Pi never ends. This atlas searches its first 1,000,000,000 decimal digits for your exact sequence, including any leading zeros.

How positions work
We start counting after the decimal point. In 3.14159, the first “1” is at position 1, and “4159” begins at position 2.

Fast, without the heavy lifting
Digits load in small chunks as your search advances. A background worker keeps the page responsive, and downloaded chunks are cached on your device when your browser allows it. A full billion-digit search may download about 470 MB the first time. Searches stop as soon as a match is found.

A finite window into pi
A sequence might not occur within this dataset. “Not found” means only that it wasn't found within your selected search depth.

The number base is your 1b.txt from 1b.zip. All one billion decimal digits are packaged directly from that file and hosted with this site. Your number is never sent to a search server.
